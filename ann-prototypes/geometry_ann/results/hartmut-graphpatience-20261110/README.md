# Graph routing + patience over 32K leaf centroids

Status: complete (exit 0). `REPORT.md` is generated by `summarize.py` and compares against flat+patience at equal held-out recall.
Remote root: `/home/sean/experiments/TieredQ-issue44-graphpatience-20261110`.

Setup: flat Lloyd 32K labels, Qwen SQ8, single-core search. Discovery is a best-first walk over the centroid graph, seeded from the entry
leaves of the nearest coarse parents (128 parents from clustering the leaf centroids). It is bounded by a centroid-evaluation budget
(2K/4K/8K/16K). Discovered leaves are scanned in exact centroid order and stopped by a fixed cap (35K/88K/250K) or by patience (W 48/96/192/768).
Models: degree/seeds 8/4, 16/16 and 32/32. Calibration (250) picks by p50; all settings are replayed on the 750 held-out queries.

Findings:
- Degree 8 with 4 seeds caps recall through missed leaves: 0.976 at 16K evaluations, against 0.985 for flat routing. Degree 16 with 16 seeds removes
  most of that gap: 0.981 at 4K evaluations and 0.985 at 8K. Degree 32 adds about 0.3 pt at a 2K budget and nothing from 4K up, for 64 s more graph build.
- Graph + patience is the fastest configuration so far: 0.963 at 7.5 ms p50, against 12.7 ms for Faiss 32K at 0.956 and about 18 ms for
  flat+patience at equal recall. Near 0.987 the p50 is 1.3× better than Faiss, but p99 is worse (patience tail).
- The graph build (240 s at degree 16) is now the largest part of index build time after the partition.
